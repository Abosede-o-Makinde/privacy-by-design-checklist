# Go-live checklist: AI meeting notetaker for internal calls

- System ID: `MEETING-NOTES-001`
- Principle score: **67.3/100**
- Default score: **25.0/100**
- Band: **PARTIAL**
- Art. 25(2) blocks PASS: **yes**

**Description:** Proposed tool that records meetings, generates transcripts, and stores action items for a professional-services firm.

**Notes:** Recording and transcript storage are on for every call by default. No retention job for audio or transcripts. DPIA started but not finished. Admin export uses a shared password today. People can still take typed notes without the AI tool.

## Prioritised actions

1. **[NO] Amount of personal data collected** (`amount`, Art. 25)

2. **[NO] Period of storage** (`storage`, Art. 25)

3. **[PARTIAL] Extent of processing** (`extent`, Art. 25)

4. **[PARTIAL] Accessibility** (`accessibility`, Art. 25)

5. **[NO] Security** (`p5-disposal`, Art. 5, Art. 32)

6. **[PARTIAL] Security** (`p5-access-control`, Art. 32)

7. **[NO] Default** (`p2-opt-in`, Art. 25, Art. 5)

8. **[PARTIAL] Transparency** (`p6-audit`, Art. 5)

9. **[NO] Default** (`p2-optional-off`, Art. 25)

11. **[PARTIAL] Embedded** (`p3-in-architecture`, Art. 25)

13. **[PARTIAL] Transparency** (`p6-notice`, Art. 13, Art. 14)

14. **[PARTIAL] User-centric** (`p7-rights`, Art. 15, Art. 17)
